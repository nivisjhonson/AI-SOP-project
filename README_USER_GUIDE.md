# SOP Checklist System - User Guide

This document explains the roles and workflows within the SOP Checklist Management System.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. Admin
The Admin is the architect of the system.
- **Goal**: Define the standards (SOPs) that others must follow.
- **Workflow (Manual)**: 
    1. Log in as **Admin**.
    2. Go to the **Admin Dashboard**.
    3. Click **+ New Template** to create a checklist template manually.
- **Workflow (AI Powered)**: 
    1. Log in as **Admin**.
    2. Use the **AI Checklist Generator** at the top of the dashboard.
    3. Paste SOP text or upload a PDF/DOCX file.
    4. Click **Magic Generate**. The AI will automatically populate the form.
    5. Review the generated fields and click **Publish Template**.

### 2. Staff
Staff are the executioners of the SOPs.
- **Goal**: Complete checklists accurately and provide proof of work.
- **Workflow**:
    1. Log in as **Staff**.
    2. Your dashboard will show available **SOP Templates**.
    3. Click the **Arrow/Start** button on a template (e.g., "Fire Safety").
    4. Fill in the answers, add comments, and upload attachments if required.
    5. **Submit** the checklist once complete. Your submission status will be "Pending".

### 3. Auditor
Auditors ensure compliance and quality.
- **Goal**: Verify that Staff have followed the SOPs correctly.
- **Workflow**:
    1. Log in as **Auditor**.
    2. Your dashboard shows a queue of **Pending Reviews**.
    3. Click on a submission to review the answers and attachments.
    4. Provide audit comments.
    5. **Approve** (Status: Approved) or **Reject** (Status: Rejected) the submission.

## How to Proceed in the Website

### Step 1: Login
Choose your role from the dropdown and enter your username.
- **Admin**: `admin`
- **Staff**: `staff`
- **Auditor**: `auditor`

### Step 2: Dashboard Navigation
Each role has a custom dashboard tailored to their tasks:

| Role | Primary Dashboard View | Key Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Manage SOP Templates | Create Template |
| **Staff** | My Workspace | Start Checklist |
| **Auditor** | Review Queue | Approve/Reject |

### Step 3: Lifecycle of a Checklist
1. **Creation**: Admin creates a template.
2. **Execution**: Staff fills and submits the checklist.
3. **Verification**: Auditor reviews and approves/rejects the submission.
4. **Completion**: The checklist reaches a final state (Approved/Rejected).
